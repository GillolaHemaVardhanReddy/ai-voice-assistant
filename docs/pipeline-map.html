Figure 2 · inside the retrieverWhat happens once, what happens every time

This is the distinction that shrank your container from 938 MB to 63 MB. Your notes never change between requests — so chunking and embedding them is build-time work, done once, and the running service only ever loads the result.

Figure 3 · the ₹0 planWhere everything actually runs

The rule that keeps this free: no model weights ever enter the production container. Local models are for learning how they work, so they live on your Mac and are reachable only when you want them.

the budget, spent

₹800/month available. The plan uses roughly ₹45 of it — and that ₹45 is OpenRouter tokens, not hosting. Render free, Vercel free, Cloudflare Tunnel free, your Mac free.

⚠️ The risk was never the monthly bill — it's a runaway. A public endpoint someone hammers turns ₹45 into ₹4,500 overnight. You already built the three defenses that matter: CORS locked to your origins, a per-IP rate limit, and a hard spend cap. Keep the cap set below ₹800 and the budget question answers itself.

⚠️ Prices move — verify Render's and OpenRouter's current terms before relying on the figures above.
