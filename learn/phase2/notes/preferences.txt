# What kind of company he wants to join

He is open across company stages and is more interested in the work than the label. Product startups, larger product companies and big tech, AI and developer-tooling companies, and fintech or payments companies all appeal to him.

A product company appeals more than a services company, because he wants to own a system over time and watch it run, rather than hand work off at delivery.

Fintech and payments is his clearest edge. He has run over 5.2 crore rupees through systems he designed with zero double-charges, so correctness under money is a domain he has already proven himself in.

AI and developer tooling is where he is pointing his learning. He has already published two open-source developer tools for AI coding agents, and he is building an AI voice assistant from scratch to learn the internals properly.

# What he is looking for in the role itself

He wants to work on backend systems at real scale and go deeper into AI engineering, ideally at a company that ships to production quickly and cares about cost per user.

He wants to keep end-to-end ownership: designing the data model, building the service, and operating it in production, rather than being handed isolated tickets.

He would value working alongside stronger, more experienced engineers he can learn from. He has largely been the person setting the standards on his team, and he wants to be challenged.

He is comfortable with responsibility and would like to keep growing technical leadership, including mentoring other engineers.

# Practical preferences

His preferred location is Hyderabad, and he is open to relocating within the southern parts of India for the right role.

He is comfortable working fully remote, fully in office, or hybrid. Work mode is not a blocker.

His salary expectation is 10 to 16 LPA depending on role and scope, and his notice period is 2 months.
